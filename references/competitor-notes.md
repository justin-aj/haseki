# Reference site notes

Fetched 2026-10-10 from homepages.

## Oliver Bonas (oliverbonas.com)

**Nav:** utility bar (store locator, login, search, wishlist, bag). Main menu, 11 items: New In, Christmas Gifts, Clothing, Homeware, Accessories & Bags, Jewellery, Gifts, Furniture, Beauty, Sale, OB World. Mega-menus per category. Country selector (UK £ / Ireland €).

**Homepage order:** promo carousel, shipping banner, "Have you seen..." row of 4 links, 9-tile labelled image grid, recruitment banner, help links, newsletter, social + review count (560,250), footer.

**Tone:** short, upbeat, imperative. "Have you seen...", "Mix your metals", "Pink & Burgundy Edit".

**Imagery:** tile-based category photos, label on each image, weekly seasonal campaigns.

**Trust:** repeated bar "Free click & collect over £30 | Free UK returns | Free delivery over £60". Review count. Pre-loved shop, takeback scheme.

**Footer:** Company, Care & sustainability, Shopping help, Legal (incl. Size Guide), Social.

## Ani Clothing (aniclothing.in)

**Nav:** Home, New Arrivals, Shop By Style (Co-ord Sets, Dresses, Blazers & Jackets, Tops & Blouses, Skirts, Jumpsuits & Rompers, Kaftans, Trousers), Shop By Collection (11 named), The Modest Edit, Celebrities & Influencers, login/search/cart.

**Homepage order:** announcement bar (Worldwide Shipping + Call/WhatsApp), hero slideshow (collection), collection carousels with Shop Now / View all, Shop by Style tiles, promo GIF strip, Best Sellers carousel, scrolling "SHIPPING WORLDWIDE" banner, footer.

**Tone:** minimal, understated. Evocative collection names ("Portrait of a Flower", "Cleopatra in New York").

**Imagery:** full-length model shots, second angle on hover, Quick view overlay, animated GIF tiles.

**Pricing:** about ₹6,000 to ₹27,000.

**Trust:** worldwide shipping repeated, WhatsApp support, returns portal. No reviews or ratings shown.

**Footer:** About, Request Return, Shipping, Terms, Privacy, Refunds, Pricing, Contact. Shopify.

## Takeaways for Haseki

- Ani proves mood-named collections plus style filter works for Indian womenswear shipping worldwide. Haseki brand strategy wants pillar/mood first, category second.
- Both repeat shipping/returns in a persistent bar. Copy for Haseki.
- Oliver Bonas labelled image tiles suit mood-led navigation.
- Ani lacks reviews and fit guidance. Haseki fit-led positioning can fill gap: size guide, testimonials.
